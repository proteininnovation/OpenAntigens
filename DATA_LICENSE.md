# OpenAntigens Data License and Source Data Terms

OpenAntigens generated annotations are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0), unless otherwise noted.

This applies to OpenAntigens-created annotations such as construct recommendations, construct classifications, derived construct boundaries, computed quality summaries, generated report text, local BLAST sequence similarity summaries for cross-reactivity review, derived ortholog/paralog comparison tables, and portal index metadata.

Third-party source data retain their original licenses, terms of use, citation expectations, and redistribution conditions. OpenAntigens reports may include identifiers, sequences, structure-derived values, confidence metrics, disease-association scores, literature counts, GPCR segment annotations, generic residue numbering, and links derived from resources including UniProt, AlphaFold DB, RCSB PDB, GPCRdb, InterPro, Pfam, HGNC, Ensembl, NCBI RefSeq, PubTator3, Complex Portal, Open Targets, and BLAST databases.

The canonical portal target table, `data/inputs/accessible_human_topology_refined_accessible_only.csv`, combines public data from UniProt, Thera-SAbDab, the SURFY surfaceome publication, and the Uhlén secretome publication. Its source and evidence columns are retained so each target classification can be audited.

Users redistributing OpenAntigens data are responsible for complying with the terms of the original source databases. Where a downloaded OpenAntigens artifact includes raw or near-raw source data, such as protein sequences, AlphaFold-derived structure/confidence data, RefSeq accessions/sequences, or third-party disease-association records, those source-specific terms remain in force.
